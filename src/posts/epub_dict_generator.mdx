## The problem

I finally made the courageous decision to start reading books in Danish. I want to improve
my Danish, and reading the news already works fine, so folding Danish literature into my
daily routine should grow my vocabulary fast.

There was just one problem: the Danish dictionaries for Kobo are effectively useless. They
exist — it's not that there are none — but the electronic dictionary material for Danish is
sparse, and the ones available are basically Wiktionary. Whatever word you tap in a novel,
it usually simply isn't there. Reading without a dictionary is hell, and reaching for my
phone breaks the flow. I wanted something quick and easy.

So I had the idea: build a small project that generates a dictionary from the words that
actually occur in the book. Some napkin math suggested it would be cost-effective —
certainly less than the e-book itself.
